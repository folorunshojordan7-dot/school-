Admissions Process

Join a community of innovators, thinkers, and problem solvers. Explore our application criteria and step-by-step submission process below to begin your journey at Winne International Engineering College.

How to Apply

  • Submit Your Application: Complete the online registration form and pay the application fee.
  • Upload Academic Records: Provide official high school transcripts or certified diploma equivalents.
  • Standardized Testing & English Proficiency: Submit valid SAT/ACT scores along with TOEFL/IELTS benchmarks if applicable.
  • Letters of Recommendation: Include two evaluations from math or science educators detailing your technical aptitude.

Admission Requirements

Applicants must show solid academic foundations, particularly in mathematics (Calculus preferred) and basic sciences (Physics and Chemistry). A minimum GPA of 3.0 or equivalent global standard is highly recommended for competitive eligibility.
