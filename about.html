About Our College

Welcome to Winne International Engineering College, where we make and share the history of premium garri .

Founded by Jordan Folorunsho, this company aims to be a reminder of garri and its history.

Our field has produced plenty garri making techniques, with our vast research of garri we came with ways to mix it with different ingredents like ground nut, milk, kilishi, beans, suya and many others to produce unique flavours.
